# Research evidence and rights ledger

Checked: **2026-11-06**. This ledger credits the primary sources behind AIC's newly authored workflow templates and scoped-memory guidance. The heading IDs are stable citation targets. Publication/version, evidence quality and permission to redistribute are separate questions.

The [research-record index](research-updates/README.md) explains how to discover and use dated additions. They are factual reference data, not automatically adopted templates or new agent authority. Keep the baseline ledger's quality, credit and rights distinctions when applying any addition to a real task.

## Reading and redistribution policy

- **Peer-reviewed research** has a journal/conference publication record; it is not a guarantee that findings transfer to a new task, model or harness. **Controlled preprints** describe experiments but are not treated here as peer-reviewed publications. **First-party engineering guidance/case studies** establish reported experience or documented capability, not an independent, equal-budget comparison.
- Prefer the specified journal/conference version over an older preprint. Do not combine results from different snapshots. Web articles are live pages observed on the checked date, not immutable copies of their original publication-day text.
- AIC distributes links, bibliographic credit and original factual summaries, **not upstream PDFs, figures, tables, prose excerpts, prompts, datasets or implementation code**. Every research entry below is citation/reference-only in this package, even when its source offers broader permissions. Templates are original AIC instructions, not reproduced upstream workflows.
- AIC's Apache-2.0 license covers AIC-authored material; it does not relicense cited publications, linked websites, research implementations or third-party material inside them. A repository license does not automatically cover its associated paper or website. Any future copying requires a separate, file/version-specific rights review and retention of the applicable notices.
- Open access and arXiv hosting do not imply a permissive license. [arXiv's license guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html) explicitly distinguishes Creative Commons grants from its non-exclusive distribution license, which grants rights to arXiv rather than a general public reuse license. Licenses can differ between versions. Unknown/restrictive sources remain reference-only; missing permission is not permission.
- Findings inform selection, not authorization. No source establishes a universal best current runtime/model, makes agreement a completion check, authorizes autonomous deployment/spending, or turns a worktree into a security sandbox. Popularity, confidence and citation counts are not proof of effectiveness.

## google-2026

- **Credit/version:** Yubin Kim and coauthors, Google Research, Google DeepMind and MIT, *Capable language models can outgrow the benefits of collaboration*, **Nature Machine Intelligence 8, 1157–1172**, published **2026-07-24**, DOI **10.1038/s42256-026-01268-y**. [Journal article, full author list and affiliations](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01268-y). This is the journal successor to arXiv:2512.08296; AIC uses the journal findings, not a mixture with the earlier preprint.
- **Evidence/applicability:** Peer-reviewed controlled study: 260 configurations, six benchmarks, five architectures and three model families under matched per-system compute ceilings. Strong solo baselines and task structure matter; collaboration can help or harm. The fitted selection rule is validated within tested domains, not a universal performance law or a rule to route every task using a fixed numerical threshold. Several descriptive coordination patterns do not survive cluster-robust significance correction.
- **Publication rights:** The article's [Rights and permissions](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01268-y#rightslink) states **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, not CC BY. Noncommercial/no-derivatives restrictions and third-party credit-line exceptions apply. **AIC policy: reference-only**, no article adaptations or figures redistributed. No implementation-code license is inferred. **Checked: 2026-11-06.**
